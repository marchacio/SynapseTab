Danger Zone

⚠️ Delete All Workspaces (Local & Remote Server)

Permanently delete all saved workspaces and their associated tabs from both your local browser and your remote SynapseTab sync server.

  • All local custom workspaces will be erased and reset to a single clean default workspace.
  • All open tabs assigned to workspaces will be closed, leaving a single blank tab to keep your browser window open.
  • The active workspace snapshot stored in Redis on your remote server will be deleted.
  • Existing historical backups in Server Backups will remain preserved unless individually deleted.
